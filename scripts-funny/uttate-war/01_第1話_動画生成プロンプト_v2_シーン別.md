# 第1話「うったて、通じん。」動画生成プロンプト v2(シーン別に作って、自分でつなぐ版)

## v1から変えたこと

- 「前のクリップの最後のコマから続きを作る」をやめた。回想の教室(子どもがいる学校)が後ろのシーンに伝染して、じいちゃんが黒髪の中年になり、部屋も学校の教室に変わったため
- 代わりに、最初に「見本の画像」を1枚作り、全クリップをその画像から作る
- 1クリップ=1カットにした。1本に3カット詰めると混ざる(墨で汚れた半紙がそのまま次のカットに残った)
- つなぐのは自分でやる。ダメなクリップだけ作り直せる
- 年金手帳は「文字のない青い手帳」にした。AIに漢字を書かせると崩れるので、「年金手帳」の文字はテロップで乗せる
- 仕上がりは45秒前後(セリフの口の動きを入れるため、40秒より少し長い)

## 手順

1. STEP 0 を貼って、見本の画像を1枚作ってもらう。顔と部屋が気に入るまでここだけやり直す
2. STEP 1 を貼って、クリップ A〜K を別々のファイルでもらう
3. ダメなクリップは「クリップCだけ作り直して。見本画像は同じものを使って」と1本だけ頼む
4. A→K の順に並べてつなぎ、テロップと効果音を乗せる

## STEP 0(見本画像)

```
Before making any video, please create ONE photorealistic still image (vertical 9:16)
that I will approve. It will be the reference image for every video clip.

The image shows two people kneeling side by side at a low wooden table in a small
traditional Japanese calligraphy classroom, both facing the camera, evenly lit.

ITTETSU (grandfather): a 68-year-old Japanese man with short WHITE hair, a thick
white mustache and round wire-rimmed glasses, wearing a deep navy samue
(traditional Japanese work robe) with a peach-pink tenugui towel around his neck,
and a human-sized calligraphy brush strapped to his back.

MOMO (granddaughter): a 20-year-old Japanese woman with a black chin-length bob,
wearing an oversized beige hoodie, holding a smartphone in one hand.

ROOM: tatami floor, a low wooden table with a clean blank sheet of white paper and
an inkstone, shoji screens with warm afternoon light, a few framed calligraphy
works on the wall (blurred, illegible). No other people. No children. No school
desks. No bookshelves.

Show me the image and wait for my OK before making any video.
```

## STEP 1(動画クリップ A〜K)

```
Using the approved reference image, please make the 11 short video clips below
(vertical 9:16, photorealistic live-action).

RULES
- Deliver the 11 clips as SEPARATE files named A to K. Do NOT join them. I will
  edit them together myself.
- Generate every clip independently from the approved reference image. Do NOT
  start a clip from the last frame of another clip.
- One clip = one continuous shot. No cuts inside a clip.
- ITTETSU and MOMO must look exactly like the reference image in every clip: same
  faces, same hair, same clothes. ITTETSU is always 68 years old with WHITE hair.
- Every clip except F takes place in the same calligraphy room as the reference
  image. No other people, no children, no school desks in that room.
- Do not render any on-screen text, subtitles or logos. Paper, book pages, the
  phone screen and the booklet cover stay blank or illegible.
- Dialogue is in Japanese. Speak the lines exactly as written. The speaker's face
  is on camera and their lips clearly move in sync with the line.
  ITTETSU: loud, gravelly Okayama dialect. MOMO: flat, standard Tokyo Japanese.
  The word うったて is pronounced "ut-ta-te".
- No background music. Natural room sound only.
- Leave about half a second of stillness at the start and end of each clip.

CLIP A (about 3s) - hook
Extreme close-up: a calligraphy brush slips from a hand and hits white paper,
black ink splattering. The camera tilts up quickly to ITTETSU's face in tight
close-up, eyes wide, frozen in shock.
  ITTETSU: 「……は?」

CLIP B (about 6s) - the lesson
Medium shot, then slow push-in on his hands. A fresh, perfectly clean, blank sheet
of white paper lies on the table: no ink spots, no splatter. ITTETSU kneels at the
table and lectures proudly. He lowers an ink-loaded brush and presses the tip
firmly onto the upper left of the paper, holding the pressure for a beat so the
bristles spread, then begins one single stroke. MOMO sits beside him looking down
at her phone.
  ITTETSU: 「ええか、もも。字はな、うったてが肝心じゃ。ここでグッと置くんじゃ」

CLIP C (about 5s) - Momo
Frontal close-up on MOMO's face. She looks up from her phone, blank and confused,
tilts her head, and speaks both lines to someone just off camera. Her mouth
clearly moves with every word.
  MOMO: 「…うったて? 何それ」
  MOMO: 「じいちゃん、それ方言だよ」

CLIP D (about 5s) - the dictionary
Medium close-up. ITTETSU angrily flips through a very thick old dictionary at high
speed, pages blurring. His face shifts from angry confidence to worry. He stops,
finger on the page, completely still.
  ITTETSU: 「ちばけな! うったては日本語じゃ。辞書に載っとるわ」

CLIP E (about 4s) - the proof
Over-the-shoulder from behind MOMO. Her hand holds her phone up close to ITTETSU's
face. He lowers his glasses, leans in squinting, then raises one eyebrow in
suspicion. MOMO's line is heard from off camera; ITTETSU's lips move on his line.
  MOMO (off-screen): 「ほら。岡山・香川の方言」
  ITTETSU: 「…香川?」

CLIP F (about 3s) - flashback, childhood
This is the ONLY clip in a different place. Sepia tone. A Japanese boy around 8 in
a 1960s school classroom proudly raises a calligraphy brush and shouts.
  BOY: 「うったて!」

CLIP G (about 3s) - flashback, yesterday
Normal color, in the calligraphy room from the reference image. The same
68-year-old WHITE-haired ITTETSU (not younger, exactly as in the reference image)
raises his brush high and shouts straight toward the camera, in the same pose as
the boy.
  ITTETSU: 「うったて!!」

CLIP H (about 5s) - shame
Close-up of ITTETSU kneeling on the tatami, head down, ashamed. He mutters the
first line, then slowly lifts his head, his expression hardening, and says the
second line.
  ITTETSU: 「68年…でーれー大声で言うとった…」
  ITTETSU: 「…おえん」

CLIP I (about 3s) - resolve
Low-angle hero shot. ITTETSU stands, draws the human-sized brush from his back and
rests it on his shoulder like a warrior's weapon. Dramatic backlight from the
shoji screens, dust in the air.
  ITTETSU: 「通じんのなら、通じさせりゃええんじゃ!」

CLIP J (about 6s) - reality check
Static two-shot, flat bright lighting. MOMO sits on the tatami filming ITTETSU
with her phone, deadpan. ITTETSU stands in his heroic pose with the giant brush on
his shoulder. On his last line he pulls a small plain blue booklet with a blank
cover (no text) from inside his robe and holds it up proudly next to his face.
  MOMO: 「会員は?」
  ITTETSU: 「わし1人じゃ」
  MOMO: 「予算は?」
  ITTETSU: 「年金じゃ」

CLIP K (about 4s) - punchline
Close-up. ITTETSU looks straight into the camera with a smug grin, says his line
and nods once. MOMO's hand enters from the edge of the frame in a tired "stop it"
gesture.
  ITTETSU: 「物事は、うったてが肝心じゃけえな」
  MOMO (off-screen): 「だからそれ通じないって」
```
