# 第1話「うったて、通じん。」動画生成プロンプト(第3版・60秒)

## 使い方

1. 見本の画像(じいちゃんとももが教室に並んでいる1枚)を添付して、下の枠をそのまま貼る
2. クリップ A〜M を別々のファイルでもらう
3. ダメなクリップは「クリップHだけ作り直して。添付画像は同じ」と1本だけ頼む
4. A→M の順に並べる。B と C の間に黒い画面「3日前」、M の最後に暗転と「つづく」を編集で入れる
5. テロップと効果音は `01_第1話_うったて、通じん。.md` を見て乗せる

## プロンプト

```
The attached image is the reference image. Using it, please make the 13 short
video clips below (vertical 9:16, photorealistic live-action action-comedy).

RULES
- Deliver the 13 clips as SEPARATE files named A to M. Do NOT join them. I will
  edit them together myself.
- Generate every clip independently from the attached image. Do NOT start a clip
  from the last frame of another clip.
- One clip = one continuous shot. No cuts inside a clip.
- ITTETSU and MOMO must look exactly like the attached image in every clip: same
  faces, same hair, same clothes. ITTETSU is always 68 years old with WHITE hair.
- Each clip names its own location. Do not carry a location, extra people or
  props from one clip into another.
- Do not render any on-screen text, subtitles or logos. Paper, book pages, phone
  screens, signs, license plates and uniforms stay blank or illegible.
- Dialogue is in Japanese. Speak the lines exactly as written. When the speaker
  is on camera, their lips clearly move in sync with the line.
  ITTETSU: loud, gravelly Okayama dialect. MOMO: flat, standard Tokyo Japanese.
  The word うったて is pronounced "ut-ta-te".
- No background music. Natural sound only.
- Leave about half a second of stillness at the start and end of each clip.
- This is a harmless comedy. Nobody is hurt and nothing is destroyed.

CHARACTERS
ITTETSU is the grandfather in the attached image: a 68-year-old Japanese man with
white hair, a white mustache and round glasses, in a navy samue with a peach-pink
towel around his neck.
MOMO is the granddaughter in the attached image: a 20-year-old Japanese woman
with a black bob, in a beige hoodie, holding a smartphone.
GUARD: a Japanese security guard in his 40s in a plain navy uniform and cap.

PROPS AND PLACES (keep them identical in every clip where they appear)
CALLIGRAPHY ROOM: the room in the attached image. No other people in it.
KEI TRUCK: a small, slightly old white Japanese kei truck (tiny two-seat pickup)
with a giant human-sized calligraphy brush strapped diagonally in the truck bed.
BRUSH LAUNCHER: a comical shoulder-carried tube made of bamboo and black lacquered
wood, about 1.2 m long, with a huge black calligraphy brush tip sticking out of
the front and a red tassel hanging from the back. It is obviously a giant
calligraphy tool, not a real weapon.
PATROL CARS: black-and-white patrol cars with red roof lights.
GOVERNMENT BUILDING: a large modern fictional government-style building of glass
and stone behind a guarded gate in a big city. Not a real landmark.

CLIP A (about 3s) - hook, the chase
Location: a wide city avenue in front of the GOVERNMENT BUILDING, daytime.
Low tracking shot. The KEI TRUCK races toward the camera at full speed, the giant
brush bouncing in the truck bed. Three PATROL CARS chase close behind with red
lights flashing. No dialogue. Sirens and engine noise.

CLIP B (about 4s) - inside the truck
Location: inside the cab of the KEI TRUCK during the chase, seen through the
windshield. ITTETSU drives, gripping the wheel, furious and shouting. MOMO in the
passenger seat screams and points straight ahead. Red lights flash behind them.
  ITTETSU: 「うったてを知らん国が悪いんじゃ!」
  MOMO: 「じいちゃん前!前!」

CLIP C (about 5s) - the lesson
Location: CALLIGRAPHY ROOM, calm warm afternoon light.
Medium shot, then slow push-in on his hands. A fresh, perfectly clean, blank sheet
of white paper lies on the low table: no ink spots, no splatter. ITTETSU kneels at
the table and lectures proudly. He lowers an ink-loaded brush and presses the tip
firmly onto the upper left of the paper, holding the pressure for a beat so the
bristles spread, then begins one single stroke. MOMO sits beside him looking down
at her phone.
  ITTETSU: 「ええか、もも。字はな、うったてが肝心じゃ」

CLIP D (about 4s) - Momo
Location: CALLIGRAPHY ROOM.
Frontal close-up on MOMO's face. She looks up from her phone, blank and confused,
tilts her head, and speaks both lines to someone just off camera. Her mouth
clearly moves with every word.
  MOMO: 「…うったて? 何それ」
  MOMO: 「じいちゃん、それ方言だよ」

CLIP E (about 4.5s) - the dictionary
Location: CALLIGRAPHY ROOM.
Medium close-up. ITTETSU angrily flips through a very thick old dictionary at high
speed, pages blurring. His face shifts from angry confidence to worry. He stops,
finger on the page, completely still.
  ITTETSU: 「ちばけな! 辞書に載っとるわ」

CLIP F (about 4s) - shame
Location: CALLIGRAPHY ROOM.
Close-up of ITTETSU kneeling on the tatami, head down, ashamed. He mutters the
first line, then slowly lifts his head, his expression hardening, and says the
second line.
  ITTETSU: 「68年…でーれー大声で言うとった…」
  ITTETSU: 「…おえん」

CLIP G (about 6s) - the hidden room
Location: CALLIGRAPHY ROOM.
Wide shot from behind ITTETSU. He walks to a hanging scroll on the wall and pulls
it down like a lever. A section of the wall slowly rotates open, revealing a
hidden room glowing with golden light. Its walls are lined with racks of
calligraphy brushes of every size, arranged like an armory. In the center, on a
wooden stand, lies the BRUSH LAUNCHER. No dialogue. Heavy stone-door rumble.

CLIP H (about 4s) - the brush launcher
Location: inside the hidden room, golden light.
Medium two-shot. ITTETSU lifts the BRUSH LAUNCHER from its stand and rests it on
his shoulder, proud. MOMO stands behind him, unimpressed, arms crossed.
  MOMO: 「なんでこんなのあるの」
  ITTETSU: 「いつか要る思うて」

CLIP I (about 5s) - the road to Tokyo
Location: a Japanese expressway through green hills at dawn.
Wide side shot. The KEI TRUCK drives slowly along the expressway toward the rising
sun. On an elevated track beside the road, a white bullet train overtakes it in an
instant and disappears into the distance. The speakers are not visible; their
voices are heard from inside the truck.
  ITTETSU (voice only): 「総理に直訴じゃ!」
  MOMO (voice only): 「…新幹線で行こうよ」

CLIP J (about 6s) - the petition
Location: the guarded gate of the GOVERNMENT BUILDING, daytime.
Static medium two-shot, deadpan. ITTETSU stands in front of the GUARD, completely
serious. The GUARD is polite, then freezes with a blank stare.
  GUARD: 「ご用件は?」
  ITTETSU: 「うったてを教科書に載せられえ」
  GUARD: 「…はい?」

CLIP K (about 3s) - not understood
Location: the same gate, beside the parked KEI TRUCK.
Medium shot. ITTETSU pulls the BRUSH LAUNCHER out of the truck bed and rests it on
his shoulder. In the background the GUARD's eyes go wide and he grabs his radio.
  ITTETSU: 「通じんのなら、通じさせりゃええんじゃ」

CLIP L (about 5s) - surrounded
Location: a dead-end city street blocked by a concrete wall, daytime.
High wide shot. The KEI TRUCK skids to a stop in front of the wall. Three PATROL
CARS stop behind it in a half circle, red lights flashing. ITTETSU steps out of
the truck holding the BRUSH LAUNCHER. No dialogue. Screeching brakes and sirens.

CLIP M (about 5s) - the trigger
Location: the same dead-end street. Red lights flash across his face.
Tight close-up. ITTETSU aims the BRUSH LAUNCHER past the camera, calm and
determined. MOMO's voice is heard from off camera. He says his line slowly, and
his finger tightens on the trigger. The clip ends BEFORE anything is fired.
  MOMO (off-screen): 「撃っちゃだめ!」
  ITTETSU: 「物事は…うったてが肝心じゃ」
```
