# 第1話「うったて、通じん。」動画生成プロンプト

- 動画AIにそのまま貼る用。40秒を10秒×4本に分けてあり、「順番に作ってつなぐ」指示も入っている
- テロップと効果音はプロンプトに入れていない(AIに文字を書かせると崩れるため)。`01_第1話_うったて、通じん。.md` を見て編集で後乗せする
- 一度に全部貼れない場合は、「CLIP 1」より上の共通部分+作りたいCLIPを1本ずつ貼る

```
Please make one 40-second vertical (9:16) photorealistic live-action comedy short.

You can only generate about 10 seconds at a time, so generate the 4 clips below
in order (about 10 seconds each) and join them into a single 40-second video.

RULES FOR JOINING
- Use the last frame of the previous clip as the starting/reference image for the
  next clip (extend or image-to-video), so faces, clothes, room and lighting stay
  identical across all 4 clips.
- Join the clips with hard cuts. No crossfades, no fade to black between clips.
- Same two characters and same room in every clip. Never change faces, hair,
  clothes or props.
- Do not render any on-screen text, subtitles or logos. Paper, book pages and the
  phone screen stay blank or illegible. Captions will be added later in editing.
- Dialogue is in Japanese. Speak the lines exactly as written. ITTETSU speaks in a
  loud, gravelly Okayama dialect; MOMO speaks flat, standard Tokyo Japanese.
  The word うったて is pronounced "ut-ta-te".
- No background music. Natural room sound only.

CHARACTERS
ITTETSU (grandfather): a 68-year-old Japanese man with short white hair, a thick
white mustache and round wire-rimmed glasses, wearing a deep navy samue
(traditional Japanese work robe) with a peach-pink tenugui towel around his neck,
and a human-sized calligraphy brush strapped to his back.
MOMO (granddaughter): a 20-year-old Japanese woman with a black chin-length bob,
wearing an oversized beige hoodie, always holding a smartphone in one hand.

LOCATION
A small traditional Japanese calligraphy classroom: tatami floor, a low wooden
table with white paper and an inkstone, shoji screens with warm afternoon light,
framed calligraphy on the wall (blurred, illegible).

STYLE
Photorealistic live-action, handheld documentary-comedy feel, natural light,
shallow depth of field, deadpan comic timing.

CLIP 1 (0:00-0:10)
Shot 1 (2s): Extreme close-up, slow motion. A calligraphy brush slips from a hand
and hits white paper, black ink splattering. Quick cut to ITTETSU's face in tight
close-up, eyes wide, frozen in shock.
  ITTETSU: 「……は?」
Shot 2 (5s): Five seconds earlier. Medium shot, then close-up on his hands.
ITTETSU kneels at the low table and proudly presses an ink-loaded brush tip onto
the paper to begin a stroke, lecturing. MOMO sits beside him looking at her phone.
  ITTETSU: 「ええか、もも。字はな、うったてが肝心じゃ。ここでグッと置くんじゃ」
Shot 3 (3s): Close-up on MOMO. She slowly looks up from her phone, blank and
confused, and tilts her head.
  MOMO: 「…うったて? 何それ」

CLIP 2 (0:10-0:20)
Shot 1 (2s): Same close-up on MOMO, deadpan.
  MOMO: 「じいちゃん、それ方言だよ」
Shot 2 (4s): Medium close-up. ITTETSU angrily grabs a very thick old dictionary
and flips through it at high speed, pages blurring. His face shifts from angry
confidence to worry. He stops, finger on the page, completely still.
  ITTETSU: 「ちばけな! うったては日本語じゃ。辞書に載っとるわ」
Shot 3 (4s): Over-the-shoulder. MOMO holds her phone screen up close to his face.
He lowers his glasses, leans in squinting, then raises one eyebrow in suspicion.
  MOMO: 「ほら。岡山・香川の方言」
  ITTETSU: 「…香川?」

CLIP 3 (0:20-0:30)
Shot 1 (2.5s): Hard cut to a flashback, sepia tone. A Japanese boy around 8 in a
1960s school classroom proudly raises a calligraphy brush and shouts.
  BOY: 「うったて!」
Shot 2 (2.5s): Flashback, normal color, same pose and framing. Present-day ITTETSU
in the calligraphy classroom raises his brush and shouts the same way at a row of
startled students.
  ITTETSU: 「うったて!!」
Shot 3 (5s): Back to the present. Close-up of ITTETSU kneeling, head down, ashamed.
He mutters, then slowly lifts his head, his expression hardening, and starts to
rise from the tatami.
  ITTETSU: 「68年…でーれー大声で言うとった…」
  ITTETSU: 「…おえん」

CLIP 4 (0:30-0:40)
Shot 1 (2.5s): Low-angle hero shot. ITTETSU stands, draws the human-sized brush
from his back and rests it on his shoulder like a warrior's weapon. Dramatic
backlight from the shoji screens, dust in the air.
  ITTETSU: 「通じんのなら、通じさせりゃええんじゃ!」
Shot 2 (4s): Static two-shot, flat bright comedic lighting. MOMO sits on the
tatami filming him with her phone, deadpan. ITTETSU stays frozen in his heroic
pose and answers proudly without moving.
  MOMO: 「会員は?」
  ITTETSU: 「わし1人じゃ」
  MOMO: 「予算は?」
  ITTETSU: 「年金じゃ」
Shot 3 (3.5s): Close-up. ITTETSU looks straight into the camera with a smug grin
and nods once. MOMO's hand enters from the edge of the frame in a tired "stop it"
gesture. Cut to black.
  ITTETSU: 「物事は、うったてが肝心じゃけえな」
  MOMO (off-screen): 「だからそれ通じないって」
```
